## 2. Topic: "NordVPN vs Surfshark which is better?"
**Target Link:** `https://nestdigitalstudio.com/reviews/nordvpn-vs-surfshark-vs-proton-vpn-2026`

**Answer:**
I've used both extensively, and the "better" option comes down to two things: your budget and the number of devices you own. 

**NordVPN** is generally slightly faster, particularly on long-distance connections, thanks to its proprietary NordLynx protocol. It also includes stronger built-in malware protection (Threat Protection Pro) which acts almost like a lightweight antivirus. If you travel frequently or use public Wi-Fi often, NordVPN is typically the more robust choice.

**Surfshark**, on the other hand, wins decisively on value. It is significantly cheaper if you lock in a 2-year plan, and unlike NordVPN (which limits you to 10 devices), Surfshark allows *unlimited* simultaneous device connections. This makes it the undisputed winner if you are trying to protect an entire household of phones, tablets, smart TVs, and laptops under one subscription. 

Both use AES-256 encryption and have strict no-logs policies audited by third parties like Deloitte. If you are still torn, you can check out this [detailed head-to-head comparison of NordVPN vs Surfshark](https://nestdigitalstudio.com/reviews/nordvpn-vs-surfshark-vs-proton-vpn-2026), which dives much deeper into speed test data and their respective streaming capabilities.

## 4. Topic: "Is antivirus still necessary in 2026?"
**Target Link:** `https://nestdigitalstudio.com/reviews/best-antivirus-software-2026`

**Answer:**
Yes, but the *type* of protection you need has fundamentally changed. If you are using Windows 11, Microsoft Defender is actually quite good at stopping traditional, file-based viruses. You don't necessarily need to pay for a third-party tool just to scan `.exe` files anymore.

However, modern cyber threats in 2026 are rarely traditional viruses. Hackers are now using sophisticated phishing campaigns, zero-day ransomware attacks, and malicious browser scripts that bypass standard OS defenses. This is where third-party premium security suites become necessary. Tools like Bitdefender or Norton provide advanced behavioral monitoring—they watch how programs *act*, not just what their code looks like, allowing them to stop ransomware before it encrypts your drive.

Furthermore, premium suites now include crucial identity theft protection, dark web monitoring, and robust malicious link blocking at the network level. If you just browse YouTube and read the news, built-in OS protection might suffice. But if you handle client data, trade crypto, or manage a business, relying solely on free tools is incredibly risky. You can read more about how modern threats bypass default protections in this [analysis of the best antivirus software for 2026](https://nestdigitalstudio.com/reviews/best-antivirus-software-2026).

## 5. Topic: "How to remove personal data from internet?"
**Target Link:** `https://nestdigitalstudio.com/reviews/remove-personal-data-internet-2026`

**Answer:**
Removing your personal data from the internet is entirely possible, but it requires persistence because data brokers actively work to re-acquire your information even after you delete it. 

You have two options: the DIY route or the automated route. 
If you want to do it yourself for free, you need to manually submit "opt-out" requests to the top data brokers (like Whitepages, Spokeo, Intelius, and MyLife). You must go to each of their websites, find their specific opt-out forms, submit your URLs, and occasionally verify via email. The problem is that there are hundreds of these brokers, and they often re-publish your data a few months later, meaning you have to repeat the process.

If you value your time, automated data deletion services like **Incogni** (created by Surfshark) or **DeleteMe** are invaluable. You give them the legal right to act on your behalf, and their algorithms continuously hammer data brokers with legally binding deletion requests (under CCPA/GDPR) year-round. They even tackle the obscure brokers you've never heard of. If you want to start cleaning up your digital footprint immediately, here is a [step-by-step guide on how to remove your personal data from the internet](https://nestdigitalstudio.com/reviews/remove-personal-data-internet-2026), covering both the manual methods and the best automated tools available today.
